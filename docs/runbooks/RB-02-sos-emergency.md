# RB-02: SOS Emergency Triaging & Police Escalation

## 1. Purpose
Guides operators and security staff when an SOS alert is triggered by a student, driver, faculty member, or hardware emergency button.

## 2. Escalation Stages
1. **Tier 0 (0 to 120 seconds):**
   - Push notifications to Campus Security and all coordinators assigned to the bus route.
   - Live location map automatically centers and pins the distressed bus with red banner.
2. **Tier 1 (At 2 minutes unacknowledged):**
   - High-priority email and repeated push alarms sent to Security Chief and Transport Director.
3. **Tier 2 (At 5 minutes unacknowledged):**
   - Automated SMS and voice notification dispatched to Emergency Escalation Roster.
4. **Tier 3 (At 10 minutes unacknowledged or confirmed severe emergency):**
   - Transport Coordinator contacts nearest police control room (112) with real-time GPS coordinates and route path.

## 3. Acknowledgement & Lock
- Once acknowledged by Security, status moves to `RESPONDING`.
- Cancellation by driver or student is strictly rejected (T-SOS-05) to ensure safety cannot be coerced.
