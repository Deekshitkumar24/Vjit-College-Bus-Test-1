# RB-12: Pilot Deployment & Driver Shift Onboarding

## 1. Purpose
Protocol for executing the 2-week, 10-bus live operational pilot (P9-T5, Decision O7).

## 2. Pre-requisites (Gated on External Inputs)
- 10 buses selected across high-density routes.
- 10 driver reference phones (Android 10+ / iOS 16+) with consent forms signed (D-06, E6).
- Student route assignments and parent guardian consent collected for minors (D-15).

## 3. Daily Operations Checklist
1. **06:00 IST:** Drivers perform pre-trip diagnostics on driver app (GPS fix, battery > 20%, network active).
2. **06:30 IST:** Shifts claimed; morning trips begin.
3. **08:30 IST:** Morning attendance reconciliation; check for unassigned or guest riders.
4. **16:00 IST:** Afternoon shifts start; boarding cutoff evaluated at departure + 15m; absence alerts monitored.
5. **19:00 IST:** Shift auto-end job runs; daily trip summary metrics verified.
